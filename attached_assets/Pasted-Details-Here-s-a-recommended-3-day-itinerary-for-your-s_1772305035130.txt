Here's a recommended 3-day itinerary for your stay at Casa Serena, blending relaxation, culinary experiences, local attractions, and outdoor adventures:

### Recommendation:
For an unforgettable experience, consider booking a **private chef** for personalized dining, and schedule a **wine tasting** session in the villa’s wine cellar to sample exquisite Mediterranean wines. You can also explore **San Lorenzo Village** for a taste of authentic local life.

### 3-Day Itinerary:

#### Day 1: Arrival and Local Immersion
- **Morning:**
  - Arrive at Casa Serena and get settled in your luxurious room.
- **Afternoon:**
  - Stroll to **San Lorenzo Village** (just a 10-minute walk). Explore local shops, enjoy artisan gelato, and visit family-run tavernas for a light lunch.
- **Evening:**
  - Return to the villa for a **private chef dinner** on the **Sunset Terrace**, featuring delicious local dishes as you enjoy stunning sunset views.

#### Day 2: Culture and Culinary Delights
- **Morning:**
  - Participate in a cooking class in the **Chef's Kitchen**, learning to prepare traditional Mediterranean meals.
- **Afternoon:**
  - Visit the **Cultural Heritage Museum** nearby to gain insights into the history and traditions of the region.
- **Evening:**
  - Enjoy a **wine tasting** in the **Wine Cellar**, guided by a sommelier, to explore over 400 curated labels. Follow this with a candlelit dinner on the **Sunset Terrace**.

#### Day 3: Adventure and Relaxation
- **Morning:**
  - Embark on a guided hiking tour along the coastal paths, discovering hidden beaches and breathtaking vistas.
- **Afternoon:**
  - Spend a leisurely afternoon at a nearby beach club, enjoying sunbathing, swimming, and refreshments.
- **Evening:**
  - Conclude your stay with a farewell dinner at Casa Serena, where the private chef will prepare a special menu under the stars on the **Sunset Terrace**.
