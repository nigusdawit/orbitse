Datahub

Help the AI understand your data. Pick a connection, then click ✨ Auto-define — the assistant drafts descriptions, relationships, and example queries for you to review and correct. Reviewed definitions are what the Business Assistant uses when it writes SQL.

Select a connection to view its tables.

Data access for the client assistant

By default, a client admin's assistant can see none of your data. Pick a connection, then grant the specific tables the client is allowed to analyze (or grant the whole connection with Grant all). Saved queries you enable in the Skills tab are also runnable by the client even without a table grant — enabling a saved query authorizes it.

Select a connection to manage grants.
