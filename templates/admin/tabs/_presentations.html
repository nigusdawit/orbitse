Presentation Decks

Slide decks the AI can launch on a visitor's screen with voice narration. Visitors can pause, ask questions, and resume.

Upload any slide format and we'll auto-convert each slide to a pixel-perfect image: PDF, PowerPoint (.pptx/.ppt), Keynote (.key), OpenDocument (.odp/.odt), Word (.doc/.docx/.rtf), or a set of JPG/PNG/WEBP images (one slide each, ordered by filename). PowerPoint speaker notes are pulled in automatically as starter narration.
